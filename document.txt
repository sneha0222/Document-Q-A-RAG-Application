Suriya is an Indian actor and film producer who primarily works in Tamil cinema.
His birth name is Saravanan Sivakumar. He was born on 23 July 1975 in Chennai, Tamil Nadu.

Suriya made his acting debut in the 1997 film Nerrukku Ner.
Some of his notable films include Kaakha Kaakha, Ghajini, Vaaranam Aayiram,
Singam, Soorarai Pottru, Jai Bhim, and Kanguva.

Suriya received the National Film Award for Best Actor for his performance
in Soorarai Pottru.
